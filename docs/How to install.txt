SUBTITS
=======

INSTALLING
1. Drag the Subtits icon onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open the Applications folder in Finder and launch Subtits.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS may show
a warning ("cannot verify the developer" or "Apple could not verify it is free
of malware"). You only need to allow it once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System
    Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
    next to Subtits and confirm.

  • On macOS 13–14, right-click Subtits in Finder, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that didn't help, open Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Subtits.app

FIRST LAUNCH
1. On the left, click "Download Whisper Large v3 Turbo" (about 870 MB).
   The speech recognition model is downloaded once, after that everything
   works offline. Other models, including ones fine-tuned on Russian speech,
   are under the "Models" button.
2. Drop a video in any format onto the window (MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM…).
3. Recognition starts on its own. You can fix the text in the list on the left.
4. On the right, in the inspector, set up the preset: font, size, color,
   position, number of lines (1 to 3), case and punctuation. Sizes and
   coordinates are in pixels of the frame. You can drag subtitles right
   on the video.
5. The "Export" button at the top right saves the video with burned-in
   subtitles as MP4. The arrow next to it offers HEVC, ProRes, a transparent
   ProRes 4444 layer for editing, or an SRT file.

LANGUAGE
Subtits works in Russian and English. Choose the language in Settings
(Subtits menu > Settings…, or ⌘,).

SUBTITLE EDITOR
At the top of the inspector, choose what your changes apply to.
  • "All" changes the common style (the preset) of all subtitles.
  • "Group" changes the subtitles of one group, for example a second
    speaker at the top of the frame.
  • "Subtitle" changes only the selected subtitles (⌘-click or ⇧-click
    in the list) or the one on screen now. Such a subtitle can have its own
    font, color and position.
  • "Words" works with the words you click right on the video (hold ⇧ to pick
    several). They can get their own color, font, size, slant, UPPERCASE
    and a highlight plate.
A changed property is highlighted in blue. The arrow next to it brings back
the common style.

FONTS
Montserrat, Manrope, Oswald, Unbounded, Dela Gothic One, Playfair Display,
Cormorant Garamond and Caveat are built into the app and look the same
on every Mac. The font library (⇧⌘T) has 43 more free fonts with Cyrillic,
each installs with one button. Paid fonts (Stapel, Akkordeon) are bought on
the foundry's site, then their files are dropped onto the library window.

PLAYBACK AND SHORTCUTS
  Space ............. play / pause (or click the video)
  ← / → ............. one frame back / forward
  ⇧← / ⇧→ ........... one second back / forward
  ↑ / ↓ ............. previous / next subtitle
  ⌘B ................ split the subtitle at the playhead
  ⌘G ................ group the selected subtitles
  ⌥⌘C / ⌥⌘V ......... copy / paste style
  Esc ............... back to "All subtitles"
  ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z .......... undo / redo
  ⌘E ................ save the video with subtitles
  ⌥⌘I ............... show / hide the inspector
  ⌘, ................ settings and language

PRESETS
You can share presets with colleagues. Open the "…" menu next to the preset
and choose "Export Preset…" or "Import Presets…".

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 13.3 or later. Subtits is fastest on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and
later). On Intel Macs recognition runs on the CPU and is noticeably slower.
